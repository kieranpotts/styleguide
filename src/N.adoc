numbers:: Spell out one through nine; use integers from 10 to 999,999; thereafter use "1m" or "3.2bn" for sums of money, quantities, or inanimate objects, eg "£10m," "5bn tonnes of coal"; but "million" or "billion" spelled out for people or animals, eg "1 million viewers."
