may, might:: "May" implies the possibility remains open: "the tower may have changed the face of architecture forever" (it has been built). "Might" suggests the possibility no longer remains open: "the tower might have changed the face of architecture forever" (if only they had built it). Similarly, "they may have played tennis, or gone boating" suggests uncertainty about what happened; "they might have played tennis if the weather had been dry" means they did not, because it was not dry.
