betting odds:: These are meaningless to many readers, so avoid mentioning them without explanation. Long odds (eg "100-1 against," normally expressed as "100-1") mean something unlikely; shorter odds (eg "10-1") still mean it's unlikely, but less so; "odds on" (eg "2-1 on," sometimes expressed as "1-2") means it is likely — a £2 bet would win only £1 plus the stake.

biannual, biennial:: Best avoided. "Biannual" means twice a year, "biennial" means every two years; use "twice-yearly" or "two-yearly" instead, since readers may not know the difference either. See also <<commonly-confused-words,Commonly confused words>>.
