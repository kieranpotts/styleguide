per:: Avoid; prefer plain English: "she earns £30,000 a year" is better than "£30,000 per year." Exception: "miles per hour," abbreviated "mph."

percentage rises:: A common source of error. An increase from 3% to 5% is a "2 percentage point increase" or a "2-point increase" — it is not a "2% increase," since a 2% increase on 3% would be 3.06%. See also <<numbers-dates-and-units,Numbers, dates, and units>>.

"home counties"; "Ivory Coast," not "the Ivory Coast" or "Côte D'Ivoire"; "King's Cross"; "King's Lynn"; "the Lakes," "Lake District"; "House of Lords"; "Lord's" (cricket ground); "Luxembourg"; "Málaga"; "midwest" (US); "the Millennium Dome" at first mention, then "the dome"; "the National Archives"; "the Netherlands," not "Holland," which is only part of the country — the adjective is "Dutch"; "Newcastle-under-Lyme";
